Md. Saikur Rahman Sun

Urban & Regional Planner • Spatial Researcher

I am an Urban and Regional Planner and Researcher working at the intersection of geospatial machine learning, spatial modeling, and climate-resilient regional planning. Integrating empirical planning practice with satellite remote sensing and hazard analytics, my work explores environmental dynamics, coastal vulnerability, and sustainable transportation systems to inform data-driven policy and resilient communities.

Climate-Resilient Planning Environmental Hazards & Ecosystem Dynamics Geospatial Machine Learning Spatial Modelling Coastal Vulnerability Remote Sensing Sustainable Transportation Planning
