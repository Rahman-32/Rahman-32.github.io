Md. Saikur Rahman Sun

Urban & Regional Planner • Spatial Researcher

I am an Urban and Regional Planner and Researcher working at the nexus of geospatial machine learning, climate-resilient spatial modeling, and sustainable infrastructure. Combining field-level planning insight with satellite remote sensing and predictive modeling, my work focuses on evaluating environmental hazards, coastal ecosystem vulnerability, and data-informed transportation policy to design safer, resilient human settlements.
