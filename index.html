Publications

Habitat Fragmentation • CA-ANN
Published • Q1 Journal 2nd Author SpringerOpen

Spatially Explicit Framework for Evaluating Biodiversity Risk Using Land Use Prediction, Landscape Fragmentation, and Habitat Quality Models

Environmental Sciences Europe (Springer Nature)
Co-Authors • Md. Saikur Rahman Sun • et al.
Methodology: Modeled 2050 land-use trajectories using Cellular Automata-Artificial Neural Networks (CA-ANN) and quantified spatial fragmentation indices using FRAGSTATS with ANOVA/Tukey HSD verification.
Key Impact: Deployed InVEST Habitat Quality integration, forecasting 170.77 km² of forest degradation threatening critical habitat for 51.69% of regional mammalian species.

30-Yr Delta Shoreline • 10,030 Transects
Under Review • Q1 Journal 2nd Author Scientific Reports

Climate-Driven Shoreline Retreat and Accretion Hotspots in a Macro Tidal Delta: Machine Learning Projections of Coastal Change to 2050

Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio • Under Peer Review)
Co-Authors • Md. Saikur Rahman Sun • et al.
Data Pipeline: Synthesized 30 years of Landsat imagery (1995–2025) using MNDWI and Otsu thresholding across 10,030 DSAS transects in a turbid estuarine regime.
Predictive Rigor: Deployed Random Forest (R² = 0.9821) and Evolutionary Polynomial Regression (EvPR), identifying 12 critical hotspots and projecting a 65.83 km² loss on southern Hatiya Island.

Machine Learning-Driven Multifactor Water Quality Indexing for Aquaculture Site Suitability Assessment: A Case Study in Khulna Division, Bangladesh

Targeting Environmental Modelling & Software / Applied Geography

Sundarbans Mangrove Resilience
Accepted • In Press 1st Author Springer Nature • Scopus Indexed

Spatiotemporal Assessment of Cyclone-Induced Canopy Loss and Regeneration Dynamics in the Bangladesh Sundarbans

Book: Mangrove Ecosystems • Springer Nature
Md. Saikur Rahman Sun (1st Author) • et al.
Analytical Workflow: Processed multi-decadal cloud-free Landsat time-series in Google Earth Engine (GEE), applying Kruskal-Wallis, Dunn's post-hoc comparisons, and Cohen's d effect size tests.
Key Conclusion: Established Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI) as the most sensitive metric, demonstrating a resilient 12-month post-cyclone canopy recovery trajectory.

InVEST Vulnerability Modeling
Published Chapter 3rd Author Springer Nature • Scopus

Shoreline Dynamics and Coastal Vulnerability Assessment in Bangladesh: An Integrated Approach Using Remote Sensing and the InVEST Model

Book: Geospatial Application of Coastal Hazards • Springer Nature
Co-Authors • Md. Saikur Rahman Sun • et al.
Scope: Evaluated 2015–2025 shoreline accretion/erosion patterns and integrated coastal exposure across 19 coastal districts using Landsat-8 and the InVEST Coastal Vulnerability model.
Key Finding: Quantified erosion rates reaching 82.9 m/year along exposed estuaries, validating the protective buffering capacity of natural coastal mangrove belts.
